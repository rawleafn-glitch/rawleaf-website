GOOD TO KNOW

A little clarity.

How do I place an order?

Add products to your bag, enter your delivery details and choose COD or UPI. Send the order on WhatsApp; our team will confirm it.

What are the shipping charges and delivery times?

Shipping charges, delivery coverage and estimated delivery time will be confirmed on WhatsApp before dispatch. The displayed product total excludes any shipping charge.

How is my UPI payment confirmed?

Share the payment reference with the team. Payment is confirmed manually after receipt is verified. Sending an order does not confirm payment.

What should I know before use?

Read the product label and consult a healthcare professional, especially if you take medication, are pregnant or breastfeeding. These products do not replace medical care or prescribed treatment.

Can I return an order?

Please ask the team for applicable return and COD terms before confirming your order.
